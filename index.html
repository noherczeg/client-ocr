How Our Free Image to Text Converter Works

Our browser-based OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool allows you to easily extract text from images without the need for expensive software or privacy-invasive cloud services. By utilizing Tesseract.js, all image processing happens directly on your device's CPU.

Why choose Client-Side OCR?

  • ✓ 100% Privacy & Security: Your images never leave your computer. Because there are no server uploads, sensitive documents remain completely confidential.
  • ✓ Fast & Free: No waiting in server queues, no bandwidth limitations, and no premium subscriptions required.
  • ✓ Easy to Use: Simply drag and drop, paste from your clipboard, or select an image file to instantly convert image to text.

Tips for Best OCR Results

For the most accurate text extraction, ensure your uploaded images have:

  • High contrast between text and background
  • Clear, standard typography
  • Good resolution (not too small)

Note: Blurry images, messy handwritten notes, or heavily stylized fonts may reduce recognition accuracy.
